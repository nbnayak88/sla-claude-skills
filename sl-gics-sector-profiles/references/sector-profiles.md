# Sector Profiles (starting hypotheses - verify before use)

## 01 Energy (calibrated - OmniVerse)
- **Value chain:** Explore > Extract > Refine > Transport > Distribute > Retail > Customer; plus power generation, dispatch, renewables.
- **Constraints:** process safety, permit-to-work, OT/IT segmentation, critical-infrastructure cyber rules, ESG/emissions assurance, environmental permits, multi-jurisdiction labor and payroll, hazardous-work credentials.
- **Workforce:** field/offshore/refinery shift rosters, bargaining units, certifications with expiry, contractors, remote sites with weak connectivity.
- **Data domains:** asset, production, market/trading, customer, ESG, worker/credential, supplier.
- **KPIs:** OEE, unplanned downtime, energy efficiency, carbon intensity, safety incidents, NPS, maintenance cost, time to repair.
- **Footprint families:** ERP/finance, EAM/maintenance, HCM + payroll, trading/risk (ETRM), ESG reporting, procurement/supplier, data/analytics/AI, integration/API, IAM, OT security.
- **Fit-Gap hot spots:** hazardous-work readiness (credential vs HR status), shift/rotation scheduling and time rules, asset history unification, emissions lineage, offline field work.
- **Risks:** safety overrides by automation, OT exposure via new connectivity, benefit double counting, change saturation at sites.

## 02 Materials
- **Value chain:** extraction/feedstock > processing > batch/continuous production > specialty/commodity sales > logistics.
- **Constraints:** environmental permits, hazardous substances, product compliance, mining/plant safety.
- **Workforce:** plant shifts, hazardous-area certifications.
- **Data:** batch/recipe, quality/lab, asset, supplier/raw material, EHS, customer contracts.
- **KPIs:** yield, OEE, scrap, energy per ton, TRIR, on-time delivery.
- **Footprint:** ERP with process manufacturing, MES, LIMS, EHS, EAM, TMS, pricing.
- **Hot spots:** batch traceability, lab-to-ERP quality, variant/recipe management.

## 03 Industrials
- **Value chain:** design > source > make > deliver > service (aftermarket, outcome-based).
- **Constraints:** safety, export control, product liability, project/contract accounting.
- **Workforce:** plant, field service, engineering, project-based.
- **Data:** product/BOM, asset/installed base, project, supplier, service contract.
- **KPIs:** OTIF, service uptime, backlog margin, warranty cost.
- **Footprint:** ERP, PLM, MES, FSM, project systems, IoT, supplier collaboration.
- **Hot spots:** installed-base to service-order link, project revenue recognition, digital thread (see OmniWorks case).

## 04 Consumer Discretionary
- **Value chain:** design/merchandise > source > omnichannel sell > fulfil > returns > loyalty.
- **Constraints:** consumer privacy/consent, product safety, labor scheduling laws, payment security.
- **Workforce:** store/hourly, seasonal, high turnover, variable schedules.
- **Data:** customer 360, product/SKU, inventory, pricing/promo, order.
- **KPIs:** comp sales, conversion, inventory turn, NPS, labor % of sales.
- **Footprint:** ERP/retail, OMS, POS, CX/commerce, WFM, loyalty, analytics.
- **Hot spots:** omnichannel inventory, promo pricing, WFM labor rules.

## 05 Consumer Staples
- **Value chain:** agri/raw > manufacture > distribution > retail partners / DTC.
- **Constraints:** food safety, traceability/recall, labeling, trade-promotion rules.
- **Workforce:** plant shifts, route sales, merchandisers.
- **Data:** product/batch/lot, trade promotion, distributor, demand signal.
- **KPIs:** fill rate, forecast accuracy, trade-spend ROI, waste, recall time.
- **Footprint:** ERP, demand planning, TPM, MES/quality, DMS/route-to-market.
- **Hot spots:** lot traceability, trade-promotion settlement, demand sensing.

## 06 Health Care
- **Value chain:** patient access > care delivery > revenue cycle; or R&D > manufacture > market (pharma/devices).
- **Constraints:** patient privacy (HIPAA/GDPR-class), clinical safety, GxP/validation, licensure/credentialing.
- **Workforce:** clinicians with licenses, 24x7 rosters, credential expiry.
- **Data:** patient, clinical, provider/credential, claim, supply, batch/serial.
- **KPIs:** length of stay, readmission, staff vacancy, claim denial, supply cost per case.
- **Footprint:** EHR (clinical, usually specialist), HCM + credentialing, ERP/supply chain, RCM, analytics.
- **Hot spots:** license/credential gating before scheduling, clinical vs administrative system boundary, validated-system change control.

## 07 Financials
- **Value chain:** acquire > onboard (KYC) > transact/underwrite > service > risk/regulatory reporting.
- **Constraints:** prudential/capital rules, AML/KYC, conduct, SOX-type controls, data residency, operational resilience.
- **Workforce:** advisors/producers with licenses, variable compensation, multi-country entities.
- **Data:** customer/party, account/policy, transaction, risk, regulatory.
- **KPIs:** cost-income, loss/combined ratio, NPS, STP rate, audit findings.
- **Footprint:** core banking/policy admin (specialist), ERP/finance + consolidation, GRC, HCM/comp, CRM, data platform.
- **Hot spots:** compensation/commission with regulatory clawback, segregation of duties, multi-entity consolidation (see OmniCapital engagement).

## 08 Information Technology
- **Value chain:** build > ship > sell (subscription) > support > renew.
- **Constraints:** security certifications, IP, export, privacy, revenue recognition for subscriptions.
- **Workforce:** engineers, global remote, equity comp, fast hiring.
- **Data:** product usage, customer/account, subscription/billing, talent.
- **KPIs:** ARR, churn, release frequency, MTTR, rule-of-40.
- **Footprint:** ERP/finance + billing, CRM/CPQ, HCM, ITSM, observability, analytics.
- **Hot spots:** usage-based billing, equity/stock comp, rapid org change.

## 09 Communication Services
- **Value chain:** content/network > distribute > subscribe/advertise > bill > care.
- **Constraints:** spectrum/content licensing, privacy, advertising standards, network resilience.
- **Workforce:** field technicians, creative talent, call centers.
- **Data:** subscriber, usage/CDR, content rights, ad inventory.
- **KPIs:** ARPU, churn, network availability, content cost/engagement.
- **Footprint:** billing/OSS-BSS, CRM/CX, rights management, ERP, HCM, analytics.
- **Hot spots:** high-volume rating/billing, rights and royalty settlement.

## 10 Utilities
- **Value chain:** generate > transmit > distribute > meter > bill > serve.
- **Constraints:** rate-regulation, reliability/outage reporting, grid cyber and safety, field-crew qualifications.
- **Workforce:** line crews, storm-response mobilization, union agreements, qualification tracking.
- **Data:** asset/network model, meter, outage, customer, regulatory.
- **KPIs:** SAIDI/SAIFI, outage restoration time, cost to serve, safety incidents.
- **Footprint:** ERP + EAM, GIS, ADMS/OMS, meter data mgmt, CIS/billing, WFM/crew, HCM.
- **Hot spots:** crew qualification gating, regulatory asset accounting, meter-to-cash volume.

## 11 Real Estate
- **Value chain:** acquire > develop > lease > operate > sell/recycle capital.
- **Constraints:** lease accounting, property regulation, tenant data privacy, building safety.
- **Workforce:** property management, facilities contractors, small corporate HQ.
- **Data:** property/unit, lease, tenant, capex/project, valuation.
- **KPIs:** occupancy, NOI, lease-up time, tenant NPS, capex vs budget.
- **Footprint:** real-estate ERP/lease mgmt, facilities mgmt, project/capex, CRM/tenant portal, analytics.
- **Hot spots:** lease accounting standards, service charge billing, portfolio consolidation.
